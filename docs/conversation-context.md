## Review verdict
**Do not publish the 200% claim as is.**

- No credible source for "200% higher conversions from responsive websites". It looks like a recycled marketing stat.
- It is misleading because it implies responsive design alone triples conversions.
- Unsubstantiated ad claims are a risk under ASCI (Advertising Standards Council of India) guidelines, which expect evidence for claims.

### Other issues found
1. **Number mismatch:** badge says "1000+ clients" but the website screenshot in the laptop says "250+ trusted clients". Make them consistent.
2. **Grammar:** "Business with responsive websites see a…" should be "**Businesses** with responsive websites see a…".
3. **No call to action:** add something like "Get a free consultation" or "Request a Call".
4. **Screenshot text is too small** to read at feed size, so it works only as decoration.
5. **Badge placement:** badges overlap the dashed lines and part of the laptop screen; nudge them.

### What works
Clear visual hierarchy, a strong highlighted headline number, consistent brand blue, a clean layout, and an easy-to-find contact footer.

## Conversion-rate guidance given
Commonly cited benchmarks (verify the current source before quoting publicly):
- Average website: about 2–3%
- Good: 5% or more
- Top performers: 10% or more (usually focused landing pages)
- Mobile usually converts lower than desktop, which is why a fast, responsive site matters.

### Safer headline options
- **A (best): own data.** "Our clients saw up to X% more leads after a website redesign." Use a real client number and keep proof on file.
- **B: no number.** "A fast, mobile-friendly website turns visitors into customers."
- **C: cited stat.** "53% of mobile users leave a site that takes over 3 seconds to load." (Google/SOASTA, 2016. Verify before use.)
- **D: soft qualifier.** "Responsive websites can significantly improve your conversion rate."

Recommendation: A if real client results exist, otherwise B or D.
