# Backlinks and local SEO (Hisar and Haryana)

A **backlink** is a link from another website to yours. Links on your own site to other places do not create backlinks, so the work below happens on other sites. What the website already does is make sure those links count: every page has a canonical URL, a sitemap, structured data with your profiles as `sameAs`, and the Hisar page has full local business data.

## 1. Put your website address on every profile you own (do this first, it is free)

Use exactly `https://www.iamsharukh.com`.

- [ ] GitHub profile: website field, and in the profile README
- [ ] LinkedIn: Contact info, Website, plus the About section and Featured section
- [ ] Contra: website field
- [ ] Instagram bio and X bio
- [ ] Fiverr and Upwork profile: portfolio or website field
- [ ] Innovaft site: a "Founder" or "CTO" link to your portfolio, and a link from learn.innovaft.com
- [ ] YouTube channel About links, if you have one

## 2. Local: Hisar and Haryana

- [ ] **Google Business Profile.** Create or claim one for the Hisar office. This matters most for "web developer in Hisar" searches. Use the name, address and hours that are in `HISAR_OFFICE` in `app/lib/locations.ts`, add your website, add photos and ask happy clients and students for reviews.
- [ ] **Keep the name, address and phone identical everywhere** (Google Business Profile, Innovaft's site, directories). Small differences weaken local ranking.
- [ ] Local directories: Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Yellow Pages India, LinkedIn Company page for Innovaft.
- [ ] Ask local organisations that already know you (colleges, coaching centres, business groups, partners) to link to your Hisar page.

## 3. Quality links from the tech community

- [ ] Publish blog posts and share them on LinkedIn, X, Reddit (r/webdev, r/nextjs), Hacker News and dev communities.
- [ ] Cross-post articles to dev.to or Hashnode and set the **canonical URL** to your own post, so the link counts for your site.
- [ ] Open-source something small and link to your site from its README.
- [ ] Clutch or GoodFirms listing for Innovaft once you have client reviews.
- [ ] Ask clients you have built sites for to add "Built by Sharukh Khan" with a link in their footer, where they agree.

## What to avoid

Do not buy links, join link-exchange schemes or spam comments. Search engines penalise them and it can remove your site from results.

## Measuring

Add the site to **Google Search Console** and submit `https://www.iamsharukh.com/sitemap.xml`. It shows which searches bring people, which pages are indexed and which sites link to you.
